Real-time video deblurring

Audited 72 research repositories, benchmarked CNN and Vision Transformer models, and optimised the winner for real-time video.

PyTorchONNX RuntimeTensorRTComputer Vision

The question. Could any state-of-the-art deblurring model run fast enough for live video?

What I did. I audited 72 research repositories, evaluated the most promising CNN and Vision Transformer models, then exported the chosen model through ONNX Runtime and TensorRT.

Result. About 5× PyTorch throughput (at reduced resolution to fit GPU memory). I presented the findings to the wider team.
