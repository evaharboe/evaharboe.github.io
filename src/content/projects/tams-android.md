**Context.** TAMS (Time-addressable Media Store) is the BBC's open approach to storing and moving media. Sony needed to understand how its cameras would fit into BBC workflows.

**What I built.** A native Android app in Jetpack Compose with Dagger Hilt that connects Sony cameras to a TAMS server.

**Impact.** It became a reference implementation that sped up onboarding for others and clarified the backend requirements.
