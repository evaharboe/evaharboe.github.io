Retreats

Retreats are where it all comes together. I've planned four for the yoga society so far, each one a little better than the last.

What's next

Next · April 2027 · Hiking & yoga

Mountains, somewhere snow-free

In planning. Probably the Alps, with long days on the trails and yoga either side of them.

After that, these are the kinds I'd love to run. The active ones keep the yoga to a morning and an evening session, so the day is free for the activity.

  1. Run & yogaMorning flow, a run in the middle of the day, and a slow stretch in the evening. One workshop on mobility for runners.
  2. Hike & yogaMorning flow, a long day in the mountains, and an evening session to recover.
  3. Ski & yogaMorning flow, a day on the mountain, and a long evening stretch for tired legs.
  4. Golf & yogaMorning mobility, a round in the afternoon, and an evening stretch. For people who'd rather not wait until retirement to take up golf.
  5. Pure yogaThree sessions a day, with workshops like handstands and inversions, and plenty of time to rest in between.

Past retreats

October 2026 · Yoga retreat

West Lexham, Norfolk

The one I'm proudest of, and where I worked out what I want a retreat to be. Fully catered with family-style meals, wild swimming, a little library, film nights and s'mores, so people could properly switch off. I taught all the yoga.

April 2026 · Yoga & island

Cyprus

A younger, more social one that I oversaw: boat tours, yoga on the beach and a karaoke night. We hired local teachers for the yoga, to try something different.

November 2025 · Hiking & yoga

South Downs

Our own space for the weekend, with hikes, yoga and bracelet making. I taught the yoga and cooked every meal myself. Next time, someone else can do the catering.

May 2025 · Surf & yoga

Newquay, Cornwall

The first one I planned, together with Blue Surf School, who ran the beach yoga and the surf lessons. A test run that taught me how much the venue matters.
