Yoga

Training

I did my 200-hour yoga teacher training at House of Om in Ubud, Bali, a mix of vinyasa and hatha with some ashtanga and meditation.

Teaching

Now I teach weekly classes for the Surrey Yoga Society, where I was president for two terms. Classes are open to complete beginners, and the focus has always been on making them feel like a community. Attendance doubled in the year I started teaching.

My classes are usually rocket or power vinyasa, and sometimes slower stretching sessions with more of a yin feel. Lately I've been leaning towards yoga for runners: mobility, strength and recovery for people who spend a lot of time on their feet.
