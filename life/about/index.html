About me

Story

I was born in China and grew up in Stavanger, on Norway's west coast, in a Norwegian-French family. Most weekends we'd head up to the family cabin to ski in winter and hike the rest of the year, so being outside was never something I had to plan. It was just what we did.

Sport was the other constant. I tried almost every sport going, from swimming and climbing to dance and martial arts, but handball was the one I stuck with, from Year 5 to about Year 10. Yoga came in almost by accident, as a way to get more flexible for matches. Somewhere along the way that flipped: the yoga stayed and the handball didn't.

Then I moved to England for university, and the mountains were suddenly a long way off. Surrey has hills, but they barely count. So I started running, mostly as an excuse to get outside, and soon found that the slower and longer I went, the more I enjoyed it. When you're not chasing a pace, you actually notice where you are. That's how a way to get some fresh air turned into training for a marathon.

Travel has run alongside all of it. I've been to 35 countries, many of them on my own, and what stays with me is never really the sights. It's the people I met and the time we spent together. That's what I want retreats to be: a group of people somewhere beautiful, moving together and leaving with good memories.
